Thirty full-page PNGs cover ten states at CSS widths 1440, 390 and 360, height 1000, DPR 2. [Dimensions](dimensions.json) and per-frame JSON record the actual geometry. Across the twenty phone frames, all **212 measured visible controls** were at least **48 px** high. Measurement includes buttons, selects, textareas, action links and checkbox labels; it does not claim to measure ordinary prose links.

## Interaction and verification limits

The real-browser manager flows at all three widths exercised keyboard Shift+Tab to the primary confirmation control and measured its visible 2 px outline. A stale manager refusal exercised focus on the error summary. Census and phone-check reasons have independently labelled controls after the duplicate-ID fix. Tests also exercised complete-page loading, explicit Previous/Next account navigation, Add attendant account, absent coverage, permission revocation, uncertain same-request retry through navigation, independent approve/reject and historical/live separation.

No full accessibility audit, physical Android inspection, screen-reader audit or daylight pilot is claimed. The browser cannot inspect phone-local queues or prove device attribution. The committed images capture fictional evidence only. Run counts and remaining gate status are in the [smoke runbook](../../../docs/qa/smoke-2026-11-19-giveaway-stage-2b.md); EM final-head gates and independent code/security/integrity reviews remain separate requirements.
