| T-10 | **Half-up rounding** for quantity-mode money until D02 closes, in one constant (`ROUNDING_MODE` in `src/shared/money.ts`). | The TRD proposes half-up; the client hasn't decided; one constant makes the change a one-line PR. | TRD "Calculation implementation refinement", D02 |
